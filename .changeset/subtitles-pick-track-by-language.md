feat(subtitles): pick video subtitles by your language settings. With the new "Pick subtitles by language" switch on, a video that already has subtitles in your target language shows them as they are instead of translating another track, and a video without them translates the subtitles in your source language. Works on YouTube, Netflix, X and Bilibili (uploader subtitles only); off by default
